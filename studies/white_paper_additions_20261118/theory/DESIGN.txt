T3 B conventions and finite bounds

The tree has edge depth d, root depth zero, a single goal at depth D,
and no duplicate state cuts. Every edge is valid. At each goal-spine
node the correct action has probability p=1-m. Its other actions
receive total mass m and cannot reach the goal. Conditional probabilities
on full wrong subtrees are uniform. Terminal alternatives end immediately.
The concentrated split gives 90 percent of wrong mass to one alternative
and the remaining 10 percent equally to the others. For branching two,
the two splits coincide and only one copy is evaluated.

Search orders by exact rational d/pi, then lexicographic action sequence.
The correct action has the largest label. The goal is tested when popped.
The counted cost C excludes the root and includes the goal. Zero probability
nodes are never popped. R sums each correct step's rank, including earlier
action labels at equal probability. L=D/pi(goal).

Rank proof. Every higher probability sibling of a correct path node has
strictly lower search priority at equal depth. Every equal probability
sibling with a smaller action label wins the deterministic tie. Those
siblings and all correct path nodes must be popped before the goal.
These nodes are distinct across levels, proving R<=C.

Upper bound proof. The popped tree at goal termination is prefix closed.
Cover its non-root nodes by paths to its leaves, giving C<=sum_leaf d(leaf).
Monotonic search priority gives d(leaf)<=L*pi(leaf). Leaf prefixes are
disjoint, so their probabilities sum to at most one. Hence C<=L.
Counting the root instead gives C_total=C+1<=L+1. This explicit convention
avoids ambiguities between edge depth and node count in the original paper.
The external reference is Orseau et al., NeurIPS 2018, Theorem 3.
https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2018/file/52c5189391854c93e8a0e1326e56c14f-Paper.pdf

The primary grid contains 640 exact deterministic cells, maximum full tree
size 87381 nodes. No sampling confidence interval applies. A mass sweep
also changes p and L. Fixed mass topology and allocation contrasts instead
hold path probabilities and L fixed. The topology comparison at binary
depth eight and mass one half is an algebraic check expected to yield
R=16, L=2048, C=16 for terminal wrong branches and C=510 for full subtrees.
This expectation was derived before enumeration. Any mismatch is a bug or
a convention failure, not a reason to alter the frozen design.
Finite staircase changes cannot establish an asymptotic phase transition.

For a full tree with uniform wrong branch splitting there is an exact
finite full-scan crossover at m=1-1/b. At equality the entire policy is
uniform and the goal is the final lexicographic leaf. Above equality,
every wrong action on the spine has probability greater than p and every
off-spine continuation has probability 1/b>p, so every non-goal leaf has
probability greater than the goal's probability. All nodes are then popped.
Below equality the goal's last wrong sibling has lower probability than
the goal and remains unpopped. All three crossover masses are in the frozen
grid. Report the first tested full-scan mass at every depth and any mismatch
with this elementary finite result. It is not an asymptotic phase transition.

this rank floor and dead end mass contrast.
